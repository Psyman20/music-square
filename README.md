# Music Square: Synthwave Rhythm 🎵⚡

A fast-paced, synthwave-infused arcade rhythm game developed in Godot 4. Swipe to the beat, build high-speed streaks, charge golden hold beats, trigger electrifying frenzy modes, and climb the leaderboards!

Developed by **Psyman** (`dum.ionut94@gmail.com`).

---

## 🎮 Features
- **Intuitive 4-Direction Swipe Mechanics**: Responsive swipe controls with haptic vibration feedback.
- **Dynamic Board Placement**: Adjustable play zone height to match comfortable thumb reach.
- **Reactive Synthwave Visuals**: Pulsing sun disc, animated floor grids, and dynamic board auras (Electric Surge at 50 streak, Blazing Flame at 100 streak, Mythic Corona at 200 streak).
- **Soundtrack & Unlockables**: Chiptune, synthwave, and electro tracks by Komiku and indie artists, with unlockable board themes and achievements.
- **Arcade Performance Breakdown**: Comprehensive end-of-run stats including reaction times, accuracy, frenzy counts, and streak milestones.
