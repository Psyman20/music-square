Psyman ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Music Square mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

Music Square is an offline rhythm arcade game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location) on our servers.

All game data, including your high scores, unlocked tracks, badges, and preferences, is stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The App integrates Google AdMob to display optional rewarded ads (such as continuing a run or earning bonus charges). AdMob may automatically collect and process device metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID), device manufacturer, model, and OS version.
  • Network & Diagnostics: IP address, network state, carrier, and general location (country/city).
  • Ad Performance: Impressions, clicks, and diagnostic logs.

For more details, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Permissions Requested

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly by the AdMob SDK to load and display ads.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide responsive haptic vibration feedback during gameplay (can be toggled in Options).

4. Children's Privacy

Music Square does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The App is a general-audience music arcade game.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because no personal data is collected or retained on remote servers by Psyman, you can completely erase all local game data at any time by tapping RESET GAME DATA in the Options menu or clearing App Storage in your Android settings.
